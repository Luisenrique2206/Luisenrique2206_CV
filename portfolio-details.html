Github repositories of my projects

This GitHub repository collection presents a curated selection of my academic, analytical, and programming work developed throughout my undergraduate studies in Economics at the Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP). The repositories include projects written primarily in Python, materials related to my undergraduate thesis, and analytical exercises developed in courses such as Deep Learning for Finance, Machine Learning for Macro-Finance, Foundations of Python for Macro-Finance, Natural Language Processing for Finance, and Asset Pricing from the Q-Lab Summer School 2026. These projects reflect my training in quantitative analysis, financial modeling, and data-driven research applied to economic and financial problems.

The repositories document my workflow when working with financial and macroeconomic datasets, including data cleaning, exploratory analysis, model implementation, and visualization of results. Several projects explore predictive modeling techniques, machine learning applications in macro-finance, and analytical tools used to study financial markets. Through these materials, I aim to demonstrate not only my programming skills but also my ability to connect theoretical economic concepts with practical computational methods.

In addition to completed academic projects, this repository collection also includes ongoing work such as components of my undergraduate thesis and other research initiatives currently in development. These in-progress projects illustrate my continued engagement with empirical research and quantitative economic analysis.

Furthermore, the repository includes my current CV and professional profile for those interested in my academic background and technical capabilities. Overall, this collection represents both a portfolio of my academic work and a record of my development as a student interested in quantitative finance, macro-financial analysis, and applied economic research.
